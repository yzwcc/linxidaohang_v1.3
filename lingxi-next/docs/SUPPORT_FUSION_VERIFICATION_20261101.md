## Implementation

`SupportPreservingFeed` binds a fixed independent support map to metric clearance
revisions. Map/receipt, metric evidence, geometry, observation identity, source
classification and counts must agree. Old/duplicate revisions are rejected;
failed updates do not consume a revision. A free ray alone never creates ground.

- Either occupied air or prohibited support blocks a cell.
- Only observed free air AND independently supported ground permit a free cell.
- All other combinations remain unknown.
- Full fused snapshots replace the StaticLayer in the new explicit offline mode.
  No secondary ObstacleLayer can clear unsupported ground or latch removed hits.
- The existing mark-only conservative mode remains the default.
- Nav2 still supplies inflation, NavFn planning, RPP control and smoothing.
  Humble inflation can convert unknown to lethal; neither may become passable.
- Ground support is an input contract here, NOT a newly implemented estimator or
  verified ground calibration. Synthetic support cannot certify a field map.
